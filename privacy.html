Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

Information collected

The contact form collects a name, an email address and a message. No account is created and no payment information is collected.

Purpose

This information is used only to reply to the message and, when requested, to prepare a needs analysis and quote. It is never sold, rented or used for advertising.

Service providers

Form messages are delivered by Formspree, a form service based in the United States, so this information may be stored outside Québec. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which receives the visitor’s IP address to deliver them.

Retention

Messages are kept only as long as needed to reply and follow up on a project, then deleted.

Cookies and demo pages

This site uses no advertising or tracking cookies. The demo pages save choices such as language, bookings or cart items only in the visitor’s own browser. That information is never sent to MT Web Designs.

Access, correction and deletion

Anyone may ask to see, correct or delete their personal information by writing to the address below.

Person responsible

The person responsible for the protection of personal information is the owner of MT Web Designs.
